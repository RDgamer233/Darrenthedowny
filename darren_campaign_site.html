Darren's story

Darren has Down syndrome and needs ongoing support and therapy. His parents are doing what they can to provide that support, but therapy comes with costs that are difficult for their family to carry on their own.

They are turning to others now because they do not want financial pressure to stand between Darren and the opportunities that therapy can provide.

If you can donate, thank you. If you cannot, sharing Darren's fundraiser can still make a real difference.
